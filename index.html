Questions before you book

What should I include in my inquiry?

Share your event date, city or venue, expected guest count and the package you are considering. If your date or venue is not confirmed, let us know in the event details.

Do you offer a 360 video booth?

Yes. Our 360 Video Booth package includes two booth technicians, lighting and an online video gallery, with a four-hour minimum rental. View the package details and ask about your date.

Can I book an event in South Florida?

Yes, we welcome South Florida inquiries, including Miami. Tell us your venue and date; we will confirm availability and any travel requirements with you.
